# Heuristics

## Nielsen's 10 (1994, reviewed 2024)
1. Visibility of system status: prompt feedback.
2. Match with the real world: user language, real conventions.
3. User control and freedom: easy undo and exit.
4. Consistency and standards: same words and behaviour, follow platform norms.
5. Error prevention: constraints and confirmations before mistakes.
6. Recognition over recall: show options, do not make people remember.
7. Flexibility and efficiency: shortcuts for experts, simple for novices.
8. Aesthetic and minimalist design: cut what does not help.
9. Help with errors: plain language, name the problem, offer a fix.
10. Help and documentation: searchable, short, task focused.

## How the skill uses them
Each wireframe ends with a 10 row check: heuristic, pass or flag, what to change. Severity 0 to 4 as in Nielsen's heuristic evaluation method.
